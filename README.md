# EnergyWise AI

## Overview
EnergyWise AI is an AI-powered energy management tool designed to help users understand, predict, and optimize electricity consumption. The system analyzes historical energy usage data, identifies consumption patterns, forecasts future energy demand, and provides actionable insights to improve energy efficiency.

## Problem Statement
Many households and businesses struggle to understand their electricity consumption, resulting in unnecessary energy waste and higher electricity costs. Traditional monitoring systems often provide raw data without meaningful recommendations.

## Solution
EnergyWise AI acts as an intelligent energy assistant that:
- Predicts future electricity consumption.
- Detects unusual energy usage patterns.
- Provides personalized recommendations for reducing energy waste.
- Generates clear reports to support better energy management decisions.

## Features
- Energy consumption forecasting
- Usage pattern analysis
- Anomaly detection
- AI-powered recommendations
- Data visualization
- User-friendly insights

## Dataset
The project uses historical household electricity consumption data to train and evaluate machine learning models for energy usage prediction.
